My Polar H10 (or heart rate sensor) is not connecting to the app.

Ensure that Bluetooth is enabled on your iPhone (Settings > Bluetooth).

Make sure you are actively wearing the chest strap and that the electrodes are slightly moistened for a good connection.

Important: Bluetooth heart rate monitors can usually only connect to one device or app at a time. Ensure your sensor is not currently connected to another app (like Apple Health or Polar Beat) or another device (like a smartwatch).

Make sure you have the latest firmware on the H10-device. You can update with the Polar Flow App.
