# Improvement Ideas for the Heart Plot App.
1. get access to the ECG data from the Polar H10 in parallel to the RR-rate which is received already.
2. the data shall be stored in an endles loop of 1 minute to allow access to the ecg data when an ectopic beat is detected or when any deviation is perceived and the ECG can help to cluster the deviation as an PAC, PVC or missed beat, etc.
3. the QRS-complex is of relevance to identify what kind of ectoptic beat happened. The QRS complex offers more data to be used in clustering the beat structure.
4. Every abnormal beat shall be stored in a list so that after the heart plot recording the user can scroll through the list and click on it.
5. When clicked on it, the user shall see the examination of the app based on the deeper look to the QRS-complex.
6. This examination shall include a plot-view of the ecg including a part before and after the deviating beat. This can be horizontally scrollable within the mobile device screen.
7. Below the plot the examination shall be explained.
   E.g. PAC because of...
   or PVC because of...
   or missed beat in measurement because of...
8. A PDF-file shall be made available on demand of the user for generate a printout for a doctors visit. That can be one page by deviating beat or several per page.
   This step is to be done only if all steps before are working properly and does not be integrated from the beginning on.
